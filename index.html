Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2025

Information You Provide

CV YAM allows you to enter personal information such as your name, contact details, work experience, and education for the purpose of generating a professional CV. This information is stored locally on your device and is not permanently stored on our servers.

AI Content Generation

We use Google Firebase services, including the Gemini API, to process your inputs in order to generate CV content. The data you provide is transmitted securely to Google for processing and is not used by us for any purpose other than generating your CV. We do not use your data for training AI models. Data handling is subject to Google’s privacy and data processing policies.

Advertisements

This app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as device identifiers, cookies, and advertising IDs to serve ads, including personalized or non-personalized ads based on your consent and device settings.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device’s ad settings. For more information, please review Google’s advertising policies at:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Analytics and App Performance

The app may use Google Firebase services to collect limited technical information such as app usage data, crash reports, and device information. This data helps us improve app stability, performance, and user experience.

Downloads & Local Storage

Your generated CV is saved locally on your device. We do not access, upload, or store your files externally unless you choose to share them yourself.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. However, third-party services used by the app (such as Google AdMob and Firebase) may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

CV YAM is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
