# Privacy Policy for CV Builder SA

Effective Date: May 2025

CV Builder SA ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our Android application.

## Information Collection

We do **not** collect or store any personal information. All data you enter (e.g., name, phone number, experience) remains on your device and is used solely to generate your CV.

## AI Integration

We use Google Firebase's Gemini API to generate CV content. Your inputs are securely processed via Firebase, and not stored or used for any other purpose.

## Advertisements

This app displays advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (e.g., Ad ID) to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of ad personalization via your device settings.

## Downloads

Your generated CV is saved locally on your device. We do not access or upload your files.

## Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with any third parties.
